Questions

Do I need a CAD model?

No. Photos, a sketch or a clear description is enough - we build the model. If you have CAD (STEP, IGES, SolidWorks, FreeCAD), send it and it goes faster.

How do you make sure the motion is right?

Every scene has engineering rules checked on every frame: gears mesh at their pitch circles, parts never pass through each other, timings match the real machine.

Who owns the files?

You do: the final video, stills and CAD model are yours to use anywhere once the order is complete.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send it with your brief and we sign it before you share anything confidential.

What if I want changes?

Each package includes revisions on the preview, so the motion and the look are right before the final render.

What formats do I get?

MP4 in 16:9 (YouTube, website) and 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), plus stills; FreeCAD and STEP files with the CAD packages.
